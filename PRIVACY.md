# Garnet Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 4 October 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Garnet** may process information when you use the bot.

Garnet operates through Discord and the Discord API. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled or used by a server, Garnet may process information such as:

- Discord user IDs
- Discord server (guild) IDs
- Discord channel IDs
- Discord role IDs
- Usernames, display names, avatars, and other basic Discord information made available to the bot
- Server configuration and settings
- Information provided through commands and other interactions
- Ticket-related information created through Garnet's ticket system

Garnet is designed to process only information reasonably necessary for its features.

Garnet does not intentionally request Discord passwords, bot tokens belonging to users, payment card information, or similar credentials.

## 2. How Information Is Used

Information may be processed to:

- Provide Garnet's features
- Respond to commands and interactions
- Store and apply server configuration
- Create and manage tickets
- Maintain and troubleshoot the service
- Improve reliability and security
- Comply with applicable legal obligations

Garnet does not sell personal information or use Discord data for advertising.

## 3. Data Storage

Some information may be stored in Garnet's database so that features can continue to work between interactions or restarts.

The specific information stored depends on the features a server uses. Garnet should not retain information longer than reasonably necessary for the feature or purpose for which it was collected.

## 4. Data Sharing

Garnet does not sell, rent, or provide user data to data brokers or advertising networks.

Information may be processed by infrastructure or service providers necessary to operate Garnet.

Discord also processes information independently under its own policies.

## 5. Data Deletion

If you believe Garnet has stored information that should be deleted, contact the Garnet team through the [Garnet Discord Server](https://dsc.gg/garnetbot).

Where appropriate, server administrators may also be able to remove configuration or feature data through Garnet's functionality.

We may request enough information to identify the relevant server or data before processing a deletion request.

## 6. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect information handled by Garnet.

However, no internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

Security vulnerabilities can be reported according to the [Security Policy](SECURITY.md).

## 7. Third-Party Services

Garnet relies on Discord and may rely on other infrastructure or service providers to operate.

Third-party services have their own terms and privacy policies, which apply to their respective services.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Garnet's features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

The date above indicates when this policy was last updated.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Garnet team through the [Garnet Discord Server](https://dsc.gg/garnetbot).
